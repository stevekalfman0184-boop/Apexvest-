## Next Steps: Submit Your Site to Search Engines

### Google Search Console

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add property"
3. Choose "URL prefix" and enter: `https://apexvest.onrender.com`
4. Verify ownership using one of these methods:
   - **HTML Tag (Easiest):** Copy the meta tag provided and we'll add it to your site
   - **DNS Record:** Add a TXT record to your domain's DNS settings
   - **HTML File:** Upload a verification file to your site's root
5. Click "Verify"
6. Submit your sitemap:
   - In the left sidebar, click "Sitemaps"
   - Enter: `https://apexvest.onrender.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

### Bing Webmaster Tools

1. Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters
2. Sign in (use Google, Microsoft, or Facebook account)
3. Click "Add a Site"
4. Enter: `https://apexvest.onrender.com`
5. Verify ownership using one of these methods:
   - **Meta Tag:** Copy the tag provided and we'll add it
   - **XML File:** Upload the verification file to your root
   - **CNAME DNS:** Add a DNS record
6. Click "Verify"
7. Submit your sitemap:
   - Go to "Sitemaps" in the left menu
   - Enter: `https://apexvest.onrender.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

## Important Notes

⚠️ **Render.com Limitation:** Static sites on Render may be marked as "dynamic" by search engines. If indexing is slow:
- Consider moving to a custom domain (apexvest.com)
- Or use a CDN like Netlify or Vercel which are more search-friendly
